What AMAN Does

Everything you need to stay protected

A complete platform for verifying any mobile device before you buy — and helping your community stay safe from phone theft.

Instant Device Verification

Enter any phone's IMEI and get an immediate, reliable status check. AMAN validates the number and cross-references it against our database in seconds.

📷
Scan from a Photo

No need to type manually. Simply photograph the phone box or label and AMAN will automatically read and verify the device details for you.

🚨
Stolen Phone Detection

Every check is matched against our database of reported stolen devices in real time. If a match is found, full theft details are displayed immediately.

🔍
Suspicious Activity Alerts

AMAN monitors unusual search patterns and automatically flags devices that show signs of suspicious activity — giving you an extra layer of protection.

When a surge of theft reports is detected in a specific area, AMAN notifies users checking devices in that location — keeping the whole community informed.
